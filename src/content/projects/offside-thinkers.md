Developed an interactive football logo quiz game that displays a random club logo and challenges players to choose the correct team from four multiple‑choice options, focusing on clear UI, smooth interaction, and engaging gameplay logic.

The game logic and backend are written in TypeScript with Node.js and Express, and pages are rendered server-side with EJS templates. Player accounts are stored in MongoDB, with passwords hashed using bcrypt and login sessions managed by express-session.
